# Final Quiz: How Much of This Stuck?

This quiz has four parts. The first checks whether you remember the main ideas. The second, whether you can apply them to real situations. The third has no right answers: it's for measuring your own practice. A fourth part, at the end, reviews what the other Stoics and science had to say. Answers to Parts 1, 2, and 4 are at the end.

**How to read your score (sum of A through O):**

- **15–30 points. Apprentice.** You're just starting, and that's fine. Choose a single chapter and practice its exercise for a month.
- **31–50 points. Practitioner.** You already apply several ideas. Identify your three lowest scores and go back to those chapters.
- **51–65 points. Consistent.** The practice is already part of your life. Now the challenge is to sustain it on hard days.
- **66–75 points. Careful.** Either you're very consistent, or you graded yourself generously. Ask someone close to you to rate you, and compare.
